Solution: Ensure your phone has active network coverage. Check if DND (Do Not Disturb) is enabled. Wait 60 seconds before clicking 'Resend OTP'. If the issue persists, reboot your phone or switch from Wi-Fi to Mobile Data.

Solution: Tap on the "Forgot Password" link below the login box. Enter your registered mobile number, submit the OTP received, and set a new secure password.
