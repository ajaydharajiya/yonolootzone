# YonoLootZone Step 5 — Search Visibility & Authority Checklist

## Before launch
- Verify the final HTTPS canonical domain in Google Search Console.
- Submit `https://yonolootzone.com/sitemap.xml` in the Sitemaps report.
- Inspect the homepage, All Games, Categories, Guides, and 5–10 priority game URLs.
- Confirm important URLs are indexable and are not blocked by robots.txt or `noindex`.
- Confirm the sitemap contains only canonical, live URLs.

## First 30 days
- Export Search Console Performance data weekly.
- Review Queries, Pages, CTR, impressions and average position.
- Prioritize pages with high impressions but weak CTR for title/description testing.
- Prioritize queries where the site receives impressions but is not yet competitive; improve the matching page instead of creating doorway pages.
- Watch the Page Indexing report for unexpected exclusion spikes.

## Authority
- Publish original guides only when they solve a real user problem.
- Earn links through useful resources, partnerships, legitimate communities and citations.
- Do not buy bulk backlinks, automated links, or exchange links solely to manipulate rankings.
- Keep third-party game destinations clearly disclosed.

## Monthly review
- Top queries by clicks and impressions
- Top pages by clicks
- Low-CTR pages
- New indexed pages
- Excluded/error pages
- Core Web Vitals
- Broken links and redirects
- Content that is outdated or no longer useful
