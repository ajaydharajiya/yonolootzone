Privacy and Permissions Guide for Game Apps

Understand why app permissions and privacy policies matter, what to review and when to stop before installing an unfamiliar app.

Reviewed by: YonoLootZone Editorial Team · September 7, 2026

Why permissions matter

Android permissions determine what an app can access on your device. The appropriate permissions depend on the app's actual functions, so review them rather than accepting them automatically.

What to review

  • Camera, microphone and location access.
  • Contacts, files and media access.
  • Notifications and other device access.
  • The privacy policy describing collection, use and sharing of information.

Red flags

Be cautious when an app asks for access that appears unrelated to its purpose, when privacy information is missing, or when a download page uses pressure tactics to make you continue quickly.

Protect your account

Use strong, unique passwords where accounts are supported, enable available security controls, and do not share one-time passwords or sensitive credentials with people claiming to provide support.
