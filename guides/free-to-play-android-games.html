Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play Rummy and Teen Patti for free on Android?

Yes. Many Android card apps and Google Play games offer free-to-play practice lobbies using virtual chips with no real-money deposit required.

Are free-to-play Android games legal across India?

Lawful free-to-play games that do not involve real-money wagering or cash prizes are generally permitted across India.

How do I stay safe while downloading free Android games?

Download from official stores or verified domains, check requested permissions, and avoid apps asking for SMS or Contacts access.
