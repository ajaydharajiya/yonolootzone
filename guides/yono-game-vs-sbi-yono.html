Yono Game vs SBI YONO — What Is the Difference?

Because both use the word 'YONO', many Android users confuse Yono gaming apps with State Bank of India's official YONO SBI mobile banking app. They are completely different and unrelated.

Reviewed by: YonoLootZone Editorial Team · November 26, 2026

1. Are Yono Games Connected to SBI?

No. Yono Games (such as Yono Rummy, Yono 777, Yono Slots, and Yono VIP) are independent third-party entertainment/gaming apps. They are not owned, operated, or endorsed by State Bank of India (SBI).

YONO SBI ("You Only Need One") is SBI's official digital banking, UPI, and financial services application, available exclusively on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

3. Essential Security Rule for Users

Never enter your SBI netbanking username, password, debit card PIN, or banking OTP into any third-party gaming app or website. If you are looking for official banking, only install YONO SBI directly from the official Google Play Store.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Yono Rummy or Yono 777 made by SBI?

No. Yono Rummy, Yono 777, and similar gaming apps are operated by independent third parties and have zero affiliation with State Bank of India (SBI).

Where should I download the real YONO SBI banking app?

Always download YONO SBI exclusively from the official Google Play Store or Apple App Store—never from third-party APK websites.

Is it safe to use my bank password on a Yono game app?

Never share your bank netbanking password, ATM PIN, or banking OTP with any gaming app or third-party website.
