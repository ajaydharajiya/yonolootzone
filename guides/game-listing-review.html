How We Review Game Listings

How YonoLootZone checks game listings, links, basic metadata and user-facing information before publishing or updating a listing.

Reviewed by: YonoLootZone Editorial Team · September 7, 2026

Our review approach

YonoLootZone is a discovery and information directory. Before a listing is published or materially updated, our editorial process focuses on whether the page identifies the game clearly, points to the intended destination, and gives visitors enough context to make their own decision.

What we check

  • Game name, page URL and artwork are consistent.
  • The page contains a clear description instead of keyword-only text.
  • The download or access destination is presented as a third-party destination when applicable.
  • Installation guidance encourages visitors to review permissions, privacy information and current terms.
  • Claims about bonuses, withdrawals, ratings or popularity are not presented as facts unless they can be supported.

What we do not promise

We do not guarantee winnings, withdrawals, bonuses, uptime, security of a third-party service, or future availability. A listing is not an endorsement of a third-party operator.

Updates and corrections

If a destination changes or a material detail becomes outdated, we may update the listing. Visitors can contact us with corrections or broken-link reports.

Editorial note: This guide is informational. Third-party apps, operators, links, terms and availability can change independently of YonoLootZone.
